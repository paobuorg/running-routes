## Course identity

The Beijing Marathon starts on the east side of Tiananmen Square, follows Chang’an Avenue and northern city roads, and finishes on the Landscape Avenue in the Olympic Park central area. This page pins the **2026-beijing-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from 北京马拉松组织委员会.

Beijing is a point-to-point course through the central axis and northern city, with separate start and finish; race-day transport and security arrangements matter. Treat the official route notice as the version authority, not an old-year track.

## Verified facts

- It starts on the east side of Tiananmen Square and finishes on the Olympic Park central Landscape Avenue.
