## The identity of the course

UTMB (Ultra-Trail du Mont-Blanc) starts in Chamonix, France, and returns to Chamonix. It is not an out-and-back in a single valley: the route circles the Mont Blanc massif on mountain trails and crosses Italy, Switzerland and France. Starting and finishing in the same town does not make it a large road marathon; the borders, valleys and passes in between define it as a high-mountain ultra.

This page pins the **2026 edition**, version `2026-utmb-mont-blanc`. The organiser's 2026 stat block lists **174 km** and **9,700 m** of positive elevation gain. Treat these as rounded official statistics, not measurements to the metre.

## What a high-mountain course implies

Distance and climb are only the entrance. The difficulty of a mountain ultra also comes from altitude change, running through the night, weather windows and the spacing of aid stations — none of which can be read from a simplified track. This page provides no segment-by-segment elevation profile and does not label aid stations, cut-off times or pass names, because those details change with each edition and official announcement, and no authoritative, redistributable dataset supports them here.

What can be confirmed is this: the route is a three-country loop, it starts and finishes in Chamonix, and the official distance and climb are the rounded values above. Training plans, pace targets and equipment lists should follow the organiser's rules and technical material for the current year.

## Same town at both ends is not a simple course

Chamonix is both the departure point and the finish, which allows accommodation to be concentrated — and makes it easy to underestimate the course. A loop passes through several valleys with different weather; conditions at altitude can be completely unlike those in town. Prepare clothing for both low and high altitude, and treat a head torch, spare batteries and emergency warmth as a separate checklist.

For fuelling, see the [long-run fuelling](/en/knowledge/long-run-fueling/) and [recovery day](/en/knowledge/recovery-day/) entries on this site. They offer general principles and do not replace the organiser's mandatory equipment and aid-station information.
