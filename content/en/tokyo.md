## Course identity

The Tokyo Marathon is a point-to-point road course through central Tokyo, starting at the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku and finishing near the Imperial Palace at Wadakura Gate. This page pins the **2026-tokyo-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Tokyo Marathon Foundation.

This is a road marathon through the centre of a very large city. Because the start and finish are separate, race-day transport and post-race departure need point-to-point planning. The official course and splits define the pinned version; race-day arrangements can still change.

## Verified facts

- It starts at the Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building in Shinjuku and finishes near Wadakura Gate by the Imperial Palace.
- The point-to-point route passes central Tokyo landmarks including Tokyo Tower, Asakusa Kaminarimon and Ginza.
- The official page provides 42.195 km splits and a course map, but no redistributable public GPX.
- Distance basis: Official 42.195 km.
- Elevation basis: The official page does not give a climb figure verified for this site, so it remains unknown here.
- The map anchor is a RunMap editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
