## Course identity

Hardrock 100 starts and finishes in Silverton, Colorado, on a high-altitude mountain loop whose direction alternates each year. This page pins the **2026-hardrock-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Hardrock Hundred Endurance Run.

Hardrock’s altitude, long exposure and alternating direction mean a single track cannot replace reading the course. The official GPX is one version entry; entry rules, altitude preparation and equipment requirements need separate attention.

## Verified facts

- Start and finish are both in Silverton; the route is a high-altitude loop.
- The official distance is 101.8 miles with 33,441 ft of climb.
- Direction alternates each year; confirm the current direction before using a GPX and never mix clockwise and counter-clockwise tracks.
- Distance basis: Official 101.8 miles (about 163.8 km).
- Elevation basis: Official 33,441 ft climb (about 10,193 m)
- The map anchor is a RunUp editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
