## Course identity

The Sydney Marathon starts on Miller Street in North Sydney, crosses the Sydney Harbour Bridge and finishes at the Sydney Opera House. This page pins the **2026-sydney-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from TCS Sydney Marathon.

Sydney puts the Harbour Bridge and Opera House at opposite ends of a point-to-point course; the city views and bridge profile deserve separate planning. The official GPX is the geometry entry point for the pinned version, not a race-day closure or aid-station plan.
