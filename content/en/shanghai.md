## Course identity

The Shanghai Marathon starts on Zhongshan East 1st Road at the Bund, passes Nanjing Road, Jing’an Temple and Xintiandi, and finishes at Xujiahui Sports Park. This page pins the **2025-shanghai-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from 上海马拉松.

Shanghai links the Bund waterfront with several commercial districts, and start and finish are in different areas. The current official announcement is a better authority than an old track; do not use roads from an old route for unauthorised recces.

## Verified facts

- It starts on Zhongshan East 1st Road at the Bund and finishes at Xujiahui Sports Park.
- The point-to-point city route passes the Bund, Nanjing Road, Jing’an Temple and Xintiandi.
