## Course identity

The Western States 100 runs point-to-point from Olympic Valley, California, along historic mining trails to Auburn, finishing on the Placer High School track. This page pins the **2026-western-states-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Western States Endurance Run Foundation.

This is a point-to-point mountain ultra; distance, climb, aid spacing and heat are not interchangeable with road-marathon assumptions. The official route and map material help with geometry only and does not replace the technical course, aid or entry requirements.

## Verified facts

- It starts in Olympic Valley and finishes on the Placer High School track in Auburn, point-to-point.
- The official course is 100.2 miles with 18,090 ft of climb; the opening section climbs to Emigrant Pass.
- The route follows 1850s mining trails; the organiser provides route and map material, but this site does not bundle or redistribute a track file.
- Distance basis: Official 100.2 miles (about 161.3 km).
- Elevation basis: Official 18,090 ft climb (about 5,514 m)
- The map anchor is a RunMap editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
