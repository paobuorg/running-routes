## Course identity

The Chicago Marathon starts in Grant Park, runs through many Chicago neighbourhoods and returns to Grant Park for the finish. This page pins the **2026-chicago-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Chicago Marathon.

A same-place start and finish simplifies accommodation and post-race movement, but a city loop crosses many neighbourhoods where weather and wind can differ. Use the official map to confirm the version; do not infer road closures or aid stations from a third-party track.

## Verified facts

- Start and finish are both in Grant Park, making this a city loop.
- The official material says the route passes through many Chicago neighbourhoods; this page does not turn it into a downloadable track.
- An official course-map PDF is published, but no redistributable public GPX.
- Distance basis: Official 26.2 miles / 42.195 km.
- Elevation basis: The official page does not give a climb figure verified for this site, so it remains unknown here.
