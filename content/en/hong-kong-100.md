## Course identity

Hong Kong 100 runs point-to-point for 100 km from Pak Tam Chung in Sai Kung, largely on the MacLehose Trail and scenic diversions, finishing at Rotary Club Park on Tai Mo Shan Road. This page pins the **2027-hong-kong-100-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Hong Kong 100 Ultra Marathon.

Hong Kong 100’s difficulty comes from continuous trails, night running and hot, humid weather rather than one climb figure. Without a redistributable official track, the map anchor only locates the race; preparation should follow the technical material and country-park rules.

## Verified facts

- It starts at Pak Tam Chung in Sai Kung and finishes at Rotary Club Park on Tai Mo Shan Road, point-to-point.
- The course is based on the MacLehose Trail with scenic diversions: 100 km and about 5,142 m of climb.
- The finish descends from Tai Mo Shan, Hong Kong’s highest peak; this site has no redistributable GPX and country-park rules apply.
- Distance basis: Official 100 km.
- Elevation basis: Official climb about 5,142 m
- The map anchor is a RunUp editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
