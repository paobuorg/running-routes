## Course identity

The London Marathon starts around Greenwich and Blackheath, crosses London on both sides of the Thames and finishes on The Mall by St James’s Park. This page pins the **2026-london-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from London Marathon Events.

London is a large-city point-to-point road marathon with a substantial distance between start and finish. Use the official map to identify the pinned version, and do not replace the organiser’s route information with a third-party track.

## Verified facts

- Separate start areas in Greenwich and Blackheath converge early in the race.
- The route crosses central London and the official material identifies Tower Bridge as a mid-course landmark.
- It finishes on The Mall by St James’s Park; the official page provides a map, not a public GPX.
- Distance basis: Official 26.2 miles / 42.195 km.
- Elevation basis: The official page does not give a climb figure verified for this site, so it remains unknown here.
- The map anchor is a RunMap editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
