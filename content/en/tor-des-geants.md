## Course identity

Tor des Géants 330 starts and finishes in Courmayeur, Italy, loops beneath the highest Alps and is officially 330 km with about 24,000 m of climb. This page pins the **2026-tor-des-geants-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from TORX / VDA Trailers.

Tor des Géants is not an overlong marathon but multi-day alpine exposure: altitude, weather, sleep, aid and self-sufficiency jointly determine risk. The official GPX helps with the loop only and does not replace mandatory kit, park rules or race medical arrangements.

## Verified facts

- Start and finish are in Courmayeur on an alpine loop beneath the highest Alps.
- The official distance is 330 km with about 24,000 m of climb, a 150-hour limit and no compulsory stages.
- The route passes Gran Paradiso and Mont Avic parks; the organiser publishes a full GPX, which this site links but does not redistribute.
- Distance basis: Official 330 km.
- Elevation basis: Official climb about 24,000 m
- The map anchor is a RunUp editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
