## Course identity

The Cape Town Marathon is a city circuit with two start areas and several waves, finishing on Vlei Road in Green Point. This page pins the **2026-cape-town-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Cape Town Marathon.

Two starts mean one track cannot represent every runner: confirm your wave and start area before reading the official route. On a coastal city circuit, wind and weather may matter more than elevation.

## Verified facts

- The race uses two start areas and five waves to spread early-course pressure.
- The finish is on Vlei Road in Green Point; the official distance is stated as 42.2 km.
- An official route-map PDF is available, but no redistributable public GPX; one track could not represent all runners from two starts.
- Distance basis: Official 42.2 km.
- Elevation basis: The official page does not give a climb figure verified for this site, so it remains unknown here.
- The map anchor is a RunMap editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
