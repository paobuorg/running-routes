## Course identity

The Berlin Marathon starts on Straße des 17. Juni in the Tiergarten, runs through Berlin and finishes at the Brandenburg Gate, with start and finish close together. This page pins the **2026-berlin-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from SCC Events.

Berlin is a city loop with adjacent start and finish, so accommodation and post-race meeting can be planned around the centre. “Flat” is the organiser’s description, not a substitute for your own judgement about weather, crowding and pacing on race day.

## Verified facts

- It starts on Straße des 17. Juni in the Tiergarten and finishes at the Brandenburg Gate.
- The organiser describes a flat city course with very little elevation gain; this page does not turn that description into an unverified climb figure.
- The route passes central areas including Potsdamer Platz and Gendarmenmarkt; a course map is published, but no public GPX.
- Distance basis: Official 42.195 km.
- Elevation basis: The official page does not give a climb figure verified for this site, so it remains unknown here.
- The map anchor is a RunMap editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
