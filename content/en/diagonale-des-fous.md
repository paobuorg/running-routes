## Course identity

The Diagonale des Fous starts in Saint-Pierre in southern Réunion, crosses the island’s ridges and cirques and finishes in Saint-Denis in the north, officially about 180 km. This page pins the **2026-diagonale-des-fous-official-course** version using the organiser's public material from Grand Raid de La Réunion.

This is a night-start tropical-island ultra where distance and climb are only the entrance; weather windows, aid spacing and private-land rules must be read in the official material. Do not scout restricted sections without permission.

## Verified facts

- It starts in Saint-Pierre in the south and finishes in Saint-Denis in the north, crossing Réunion point-to-point.
- The official distance is about 180 km with about 10,200 m of climb; the route crosses the Pitons, Cirques and Remparts national park.
- The organiser prohibits scouting on some private-land sections and publishes no redistributable GPX; this site provides only an anchor and official link.
- Distance basis: Official about 180 km.
- Elevation basis: Official climb about 10,200 m
- The map anchor is a RunUp editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
