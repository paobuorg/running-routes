## Course identity

The New York City Marathon starts at Fort Wadsworth on Staten Island, crosses all five boroughs and finishes in Central Park, Manhattan. This page pins the **2025-tcs-new-york-city-marathon** version using the organiser's public material from NYRR.

The island start and five-borough route make logistics more complex than a same-city loop. Do not use an old track to plan transport or start timing; follow the current official instructions.

## Verified facts

- It starts at Fort Wadsworth on Staten Island and finishes in Central Park, Manhattan.
- The route crosses all five boroughs and is point-to-point.
- This page pins the 2025 version; current route changes and race-day closures must be checked on the official page.
- Distance basis: Official 26.2 miles / 42.195 km.
- Elevation basis: The official page does not give a climb figure verified for this site, so it remains unknown here.
- The map anchor is a RunMap editorial overview location, not an official start, finish or course coordinate.
